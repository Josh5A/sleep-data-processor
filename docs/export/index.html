Exporting from Sleep Cycle

The export is five taps deep, in a menu you have to know where to find. Here is the path on iOS, with a picture of each screen. Android should be similar.

Time: about a minute. The red ring on each picture is the thing to tap.

You can also view Sleep Cycle's instructions here.

  1. Open Sleep Cycle and tap Profile

    It is the last tab in the row along the bottom, on the right.

  2. Scroll down and tap “••• More”

    Profile opens on your totals — nights recorded, average time, backup. Keep scrolling until ou reach the “More” row, under Weekly report.

  3. Tap Account

    First row on the More screen, under the heading Personal.

  4. Tap Export data

    Fourth in the list of blue links, under Log out.

    Read the row before you tap it. The two rows underneath are red and they erase your sleep history. Export data is the last of the blue ones.

  5. Send yourself the file

    Sleep Cycle builds the file — sleepdata.csv, a plain text file, a couple of hundred kilobytes — and hands it to the usual iOS share sheet. Two of those options are the ones you want:

    • — AirDrop, if you are going to finish on a Mac. The file lands in Downloads, then you can format it with Chart My Sleep.
    • — Copy, which puts the contents of the file on your clipboard. Paste it straight into the box here to process it. If your Mac and your phone are signed in to the same Apple account, a copy from the phone can be pasted on the Mac.

    Every export is your whole history, not just the nights since last time. That is why our tool tells you to paste over the top of the old rows rather than underneath them.

Congrats, you've exported your sleep data

Drop it into the box on the tool, or paste the text in. The rows come back formatted for the chart template, and nothing in the file leaves your own device.
